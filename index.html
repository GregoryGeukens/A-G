How it works

Four steps. The first two happen before you hear from us.

  1. We find you

    We look for businesses and people whose current website is dated, slow or difficult to use.

  2. We build it

    We design and build a new version with the content from your current site, before we contact you.

  3. We get in touch

    You get a short message with a link to your new website, ready to open and click through.

  4. You decide

    If you like it, we talk about putting it live. If not, you keep the link and we leave it there.

What you get

A website that does its basic job well.

  • Works on every screen

    One design that adapts to phones, tablets and computers, so nothing is cut off or too small to read.

  • Loads quickly

    Light pages without heavy extras, so visitors don't leave while they wait.

  • Easy to find on Google

    Clear page titles and descriptions, so search engines understand what you do.

  • A clear way to reach you

    Phone number, address, opening hours and a contact option, placed where people look for them.

  • Your own words and photos

    We start from what your current site already says and tidy it up instead of replacing it.
